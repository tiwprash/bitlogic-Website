Why Do Most Crypto Scanners Fail for US Traders?

Most scanner apps work the same way: your phone downloads market data straight from the exchange and checks it against your rule. That has two weaknesses for people in the US.

  • Binance.com refuses US connections. A phone on a US internet connection is turned away, so the scan never loads.
  • Phones cannot watch the market all day. iPhones stop apps from scanning in the background, and Android phones throttle them to save battery. A scanner that lives on your phone misses setups while the app is closed.

BitLogic moves the watching off your phone and onto a server.

Can I Scan Binance.US From the US?

Yes. Binance.US is a separate US company (BAM Trading Services) that offers spot trading to US residents in supported states. BitLogic reads Binance.US directly, so a scan you run yourself works from any US connection.
