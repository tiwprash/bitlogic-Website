How to Screen Crypto on CoinDCX (Step-by-Step Guide)

CoinDCX lists hundreds of trading pairs. Checking each one by hand for an RSI reversal or a volume breakout is impossible: by the time you reach the bottom of the list, the setups at the top have already moved.

A screener solves this. Instead of reading charts one at a time, you describe the setup once and let software test every CoinDCX pair against it.

This guide shows you exactly how to screen crypto on CoinDCX using BitLogic, a free crypto screener app (this walkthrough uses the Android version; a Windows version is also available). You will build a strategy from scratch, scan the CoinDCX market with it, and set up alerts so you are told when a setup appears, including alerts delivered straight to Telegram.

Get started: Download BitLogic free on Google Play, or explore the scanner first in the free web screener.

Why Screen CoinDCX Instead of Watching Charts

Manual chart-watching fails for three reasons:

  • Scale. CoinDCX supports hundreds of spot and futures pairs. Nobody checks them all.
  • Timing. A 15-minute breakout does not wait for you to scroll to it.
  • Consistency. Your rules drift when you are tired. Software applies identical rules every scan.

A screener flips this around: you define the conditions, and the app searches the whole exchange. You only look at charts that already match your setup.

How to Screen Crypto on CoinDCX: Step by Step

Step 1: Select CoinDCX and Your Market

Open the Strategy tab and choose Build My Own. At the top, set:

  • Exchange: CoinDCX
  • Market: Spot or Futures
  • Strategy Mode: Single Timeframe (or Multi-Timeframe for confirmation across two)
  • Candles: the timeframe you trade, for example 1h

There is also a Volume Filter. Setting a minimum 24-hour volume is one of the most useful filters on any exchange: it removes thin pairs where a signal is noise rather than opportunity.

Step 2: Add Your Entry Conditions

Scroll to Signal Rules (Entry Points). You get a Long Position block for buy conditions and a Short Position block for sell conditions.

Tap Add Indicator and search the library. BitLogic includes the standard technical toolkit (RSI, EMA, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic RSI, SuperTrend, VWAP, Ichimoku, OBV), plus candlestick patterns such as Hammer, Engulfing and Doji, and raw price action like Close, High, Low and Volume. You can see the full breakdown in our supported indicators overview.

Pick Relative Strength Index. Each indicator has its own settings: period, price source, and which candle to read.

Candle Offset deserves attention. "Last Closed" evaluates the last completed candle, which is what you want for a rule that should not change mid-candle. Reading the live candle instead means a condition can flip repeatedly before the candle closes.

Save, then choose the comparison (Is Less Than, value 30) and the finished rule reads back in plain language:

Add a second condition (say, price above its 200 EMA) to require a trend filter, and a pair must satisfy both before it is reported. That is the same idea behind our RSI Reversal Bounce strategy, which pairs an RSI cross with a 50 EMA trend filter and volume confirmation.

Step 3: Set Your Exits

Below the rules, Risk & Profit Management holds your Take Profit and Stop Loss, either as fixed percentages or as conditional exits driven by indicators. Define exits before you scan. Deciding them after you are in a position is how small losses become large ones.

Step 4: Run the Scan

Name the strategy, tap Save, then tap Scan Market Now. BitLogic pulls candles for every CoinDCX pair that passes your volume filter, evaluates your rules against each one, and returns only the matches.

Set Alerts So You Stop Watching the Screen

Screening tells you what is happening now. Alerts tell you the moment it happens.

The Alerts tab carries five market-wide feeds, each with its own threshold (see the full list of BitLogic alert types):

  • Liquidation Monitor: whale liquidations, live from Binance
  • Volume Spikes: candle volume far above its recent average
  • Price Anomalies: sudden pumps and dumps on high-volume pairs
  • Funding Rates: extreme funding, when positioning is heavily one-sided
  • Open Interest: positions opening or unwinding fast

Note the distinction: these five feeds track market-wide activity across major derivatives venues, which is useful context for any market including CoinDCX. Alerts for your own strategy on CoinDCX come from Live Monitoring, next.

Tap any feed to set its threshold, for example liquidations over $10,000, or a volume spike over 3× average on the 15-minute candle. Set them where they are meaningful to you; a threshold too low turns a useful feed into noise.

Run Live Monitoring on CoinDCX

A manual scan is a snapshot. Live Monitoring runs your strategy on repeat in the background. Open the Monitoring tab, pick your saved strategy, set Exchange to CoinDCX, choose the market, and start it.

  • Run up to 5 strategies at once
  • Choose your scan interval to match your timeframe
  • Scanning continues when the app is closed, and you get a push notification when a setup triggers

The screen also runs a Phone Setup check. This matters more than it looks: Android aggressively suspends background apps, so if battery optimization is on, scanning stops and your alerts stop with it. If the panel flags something, fix it. Otherwise monitoring will quietly die a few hours in.
