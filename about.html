About BitLogic

Zero-code automation for crypto traders.

Who We Are

BitLogic was built to make algorithmic-style market scanning accessible without requiring any programming knowledge. Traders who need to monitor multiple timeframes across many coins at once have always faced a trade-off: sit glued to charts, or learn to code. BitLogic removes that trade-off.

Our team of software engineers and crypto traders built BitLogic to be a no-code crypto strategy builder and real-time market screener, giving everyday traders a visual way to define and automate the exact setups they look for.

Our Mission

We believe technology should serve the trader, not complicate their workflow. BitLogic gives you access to a wide range of technical indicators and real-time scanning through an intuitive, visual interface. Whether you're tracking a simple RSI divergence or a Bollinger Band squeeze combined with a MACD crossover, BitLogic does the scanning so you can focus on strategy.

Why Trust Us?

Our scanning engines connect directly to top-tier exchanges — Binance, Bybit, CoinDCX, Kraken, and OKX — to keep the data you see accurate and current. We continue refining our scanning pipeline to minimize latency, so that when your strategy's conditions are met, you get a notification as close to instantly as possible.

We do not manage your funds, ask for your private keys, or execute trades automatically on your behalf. BitLogic is, at its core, an analytical tool designed to support your decision-making — not replace it.
