How the screener works

1. Choose your conditions. Pick indicators, price action or any of the 64 candlestick patterns, and join them with AND/OR logic. A setup can be as simple as "RSI crosses above 30" or as specific as "EMA 9 above EMA 21, price above EMA 200, and volume above its 20-period average."

2. Pick exchanges and timeframes. Scan Binance, Bybit, CoinDCX, OKX, KuCoin, Kraken, Bitstamp, VALR and Upbit, across timeframes from 1 minute to 1 day at the same time. If you would rather not start from scratch, deploy one of the prebuilt strategies in one tap, such as the RSI reversal bounce or the triple EMA cross, and adjust it to your risk profile. You can browse them all in our strategy library.

3. Get notified. Matching coins appear in the screener instantly. In the Android and Windows apps, BitLogic keeps checking in the background and sends a push notification the moment a setup fires, so you don't have to sit in front of charts.

Crypto screener FAQ

Is the BitLogic crypto screener really free?

Yes. The web screener is free in your browser, and all six alert types in the app are free. The free app is supported by ads. Guests get 3 scans per 24 hours, and a free account raises that limit. BitLogic Pro adds Telegram delivery, unlimited scans, an ad-free experience and priority support.

Do I need an account or to connect my exchange?

No. Guest mode lets you start scanning without signing up; guests get 3 scans per 24 hours, and a free account raises that limit. BitLogic never asks for your exchange API private keys and never touches your funds.

What is the difference between the web screener and the app?

The web screener is for manual scanning in your browser. The Android and Windows apps add background monitoring and push notifications, so alerts reach you even when the app is closed.

Which exchanges does BitLogic support?

Binance, Bybit, CoinDCX, OKX, KuCoin, Kraken, Bitstamp, VALR and Upbit.

Can I use BitLogic as a CoinDCX futures scanner?

Yes. CoinDCX is a supported exchange, so you can scan its pairs with the same indicator, volume and momentum conditions you use elsewhere. Our step-by-step CoinDCX screening guide walks through it.

Does BitLogic give trading advice or place trades?

No. BitLogic is an analysis and alerting tool. It does not provide financial advice and does not execute trades on your behalf.
