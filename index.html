Frequently Asked Questions

Is BitLogic free to use?

Yes. All six alert types and the screener are free on Android and Windows, and you can try the core screener on this website. The free app is supported by ads. Guests get 3 scans per 24 hours, and a free account raises that limit. BitLogic Pro adds Telegram delivery, unlimited scans, an ad-free experience and priority support.

Do I need to know how to code to use BitLogic?

No coding experience is required. BitLogic is a no-code crypto strategy builder where you combine conditions with AND/OR logic.

Which exchanges are supported?

BitLogic supports real-time market scanning for Binance, Bybit, Coinbase, CoinDCX, OKX, KuCoin, Kraken, Bitstamp, VALR and Upbit.

Do I need to create an account to try BitLogic?

No. BitLogic offers a frictionless Guest Mode so you can start scanning instantly without signing up or providing an email. Guests get 3 scans per 24 hours; creating a free account raises that limit.

Does BitLogic ask for my exchange API keys or handle my funds?

No. BitLogic never asks for your exchange API private keys and never touches your funds.

Does BitLogic give financial advice or trade for me?

No. BitLogic is a technical analysis and market-scanning tool. It does not provide financial advice and does not execute trades on your behalf.
