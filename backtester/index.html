BitLogic Backtester FAQ

What is BitLogic Backtester?

BitLogic Backtester is a no-code backtesting app for crypto and stocks. You build a trading strategy from 60+ indicators and patterns, test it on years of real market data with fees and slippage included, optimize it with walk-forward testing, and paper trade it on live prices. It is available on Windows from the Microsoft Store, with Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android versions coming soon.

Can I backtest a trading strategy without coding?

Yes. You pick indicators, patterns and conditions from menus and combine them with AND and OR rules. There is no code or scripting language to learn, and 16 ready-made templates give you a starting point.

Which markets can I backtest?

Crypto spot and futures, and stocks from the US, India, the UK, Germany, Hong Kong, Canada, Australia and Japan, on timeframes from 1 minute to 1 week.

Is BitLogic Backtester free?

You can start free: a free BitLogic account includes 3 backtests and 1 optimization. A Week pass costs US$3.50 one-time for every feature for 7 days. The Monthly (US$4.99) and Annual (US$49.99) plans include every Backtester feature plus BitLogic Pro for the crypto screener app.

I already pay for BitLogic Pro. Do I get the Backtester?

Yes. Sign in to BitLogic Backtester with the same BitLogic account and the Backtester is included in a paid BitLogic Pro membership.

Is BitLogic Backtester on Mac, iPhone or Android?

Not yet. It is available on Windows from the Microsoft Store today, and Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android versions are coming soon.

How realistic are the backtest results?

Backtests include trading fees, slippage and, for futures, funding payments. Reports show drawdown, risk ratios and Monte Carlo analysis, and the app tells you plainly when a strategy did worse than simply buying and holding.

What is walk-forward testing?

Walk-forward testing picks the best settings on one period of data and then checks them on a later period the optimizer never saw. Settings that only worked on past data by luck tend to fail that check, which helps you avoid curve-fitting.

Can I paper trade a strategy before using real money?

Yes. Paper trading runs your strategy on live prices with virtual money, so you can watch how it behaves in real time without any risk.

Does BitLogic Backtester place real trades or give advice?

No. It is an analysis tool for education and research. It does not place real trades and does not give financial advice. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

How is the Backtester different from the BitLogic screener?

The BitLogic screener scans live markets and alerts you when a coin matches your rules today. The Backtester tests how a set of rules would have performed over years of past data. Many traders use the screener to find setups and the Backtester to check them first.
