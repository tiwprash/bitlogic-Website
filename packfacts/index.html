PackFacts

Food label scanner: one scan, checked for everyone you shop for.

Point your camera at a packaged-food label and PackFacts checks it against each person in your household: allergies, vegetarian and vegan diets, halal, kosher, Jain and Hindu rules, and health needs such as diabetes or high blood pressure. Labels are read on your phone; there is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Support

Email support@bitlogic.info. Please include your phone model and, if a label read badly, a photo of the label.

Common questions

Is it medical advice? No. PackFacts is an informational tool. For a severe allergy always read the packaging yourself and contact the manufacturer when in doubt.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Why are there ads? PackFacts is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. You can change ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy choices where that applies.
