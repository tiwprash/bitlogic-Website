Privacy Policy

PackFacts does not require an account and does not operate a server. Your scan photos, ingredient history, and preferences stay on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or have access to your name, email, or any account information (there is no account system), the photos you scan, your ingredient/allergy/medical/diet preferences, or your scan history. All of this stays in the app's private local storage on your device.

Permissions we request

Camera — to photograph a label to scan; images are processed on-device and never transmitted anywhere. Photos/media — the system photo picker shares only the specific photo you choose, not your whole library. Internet — required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads; the app's scanning and analysis features work fully offline.

Advertising

PackFacts is free and shows a banner ad, plus a full-screen ad after every third scan, via Google AdMob. We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent for personalized ads where required by your region's law (e.g. EU/UK). Google and its ad partners may collect your advertising identifier, approximate IP-based location, and device/usage data to serve and measure ads — this is handled by Google, not by us. On iOS we show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used, and ads stay non-personalised if you decline. Where consent is required you can change your ad choices at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-party services

Google ML Kit performs text recognition entirely on your device — no image or text is sent to Google or to us for this feature. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform handle ads and consent as described above. We use no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data retention & deletion

Because everything is stored locally, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it permanently deletes your data. We have no copy to retain, because we never receive it.

Children's privacy

PackFacts is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about PackFacts or this policy: support@bitlogic.info. PackFacts is published by BitLogic.
