What PackFacts does

PackFacts scans product labels using your camera or photo library, extracts the ingredient list on-device, and checks it against a local ingredient database and the preferences you choose to save. The app is free and supported by banner ads.

Accuracy

Text recognition can misread labels, and the ingredient database doesn't cover every ingredient that exists — unrecognized items are shown as-is rather than guessed at. "% Daily Value" figures use general FDA/WHO reference values for a 2,000-calorie diet, not a personalized recommendation.
