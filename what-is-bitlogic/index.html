What is BitLogic?

Quick answer. BitLogic is a no-code crypto screener and alert app for Android and Windows, with a free web screener. It scans hundreds of coins on 11 exchanges against rules you build and sends matches as push notifications or Telegram messages. It does not place trades, hold funds or ask for exchange API keys, and it gives no financial advice.

Get BitLogic free: Google Play (Android), Microsoft Store (Windows), or try the web screener first.

What does BitLogic do?

BitLogic checks market data against rules you set. You choose an exchange and a market, add conditions such as "RSI is less than 35" or "price crosses above the 50-period EMA", and press scan. BitLogic returns every pair that matches, with a calculated entry, target and stop level based on your own take-profit and stop-loss settings. You can also turn on five free market alert feeds, or run a strategy as a live monitor.

Is BitLogic free?

Yes. The app is free to download, scanning is free, and the five market alert feeds are free. Guests get 3 scans per 24 hours; a free account removes the limit. Free users start a live monitor after watching one short ad (one ad unlocks two monitors). The optional BitLogic Pro subscription removes ads, delivers alerts to Telegram, starts monitors without an ad and adds priority support, for US$4.99/month or US$49.99/year with a 14-day free trial.

Does BitLogic trade for me or need my exchange API keys?

No. BitLogic reads public market data. It does not connect to your exchange account, never asks for API keys, does not hold or move funds and does not place orders.

Which exchanges does BitLogic support?

Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, Binance.US, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Bitstamp, Upbit, VALR and CoinDCX. Whether an exchange's data is reachable depends on your country: for example Binance and Bybit block connections from the United States, so US users choose Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp or Binance.US.

What alerts does BitLogic send?

Five free market feeds use Binance Futures market data and are sent from BitLogic's servers: liquidation alerts (large forced liquidations), funding rate alerts (extreme funding), open interest alerts (fast changes in open interest), volume spike alerts (volume far above its recent average) and price move alerts (sudden pumps and dumps on high-volume pairs). The sixth type, strategy alerts, uses the exchange you pick and fires when your own rules match. The app keeps a 24-hour history of the alerts you received.

How does live monitoring work?

You pick a strategy, an exchange and a market, and BitLogic keeps re-checking it in the background. When your rules match, you get a push notification, even when the app is closed, and every scan sends all the matching signals. You can run up to five monitors at once.

Which indicators does BitLogic have?

More than 60 technical indicators (for example RSI, SMA, EMA, MACD, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, ADX, Stochastic, ATR and OBV) and more than 60 candlestick patterns (for example engulfing, hammer, doji and morning star). Indicator values follow the standard TA-Lib method that TradingView also uses. Rules can be combined with AND/OR and set for long or short positions.

Does BitLogic work in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and Germany?

The app can be installed in all five. Which exchanges work depends on the country: Coinbase and Kraken serve all five markets; Binance.US is for US users; Bitstamp serves the US, the UK and Germany; OKX, Bybit and KuCoin are mainly used in the UK and Australia. BitLogic does not recommend an exchange and does not check whether you may use one, so check your local rules.

What are BitLogic's limits?

It is an information tool, not financial advice, and it does not execute trades. It has no price charts. The interface is English only. The five free market alert feeds use Binance Futures market data only. Guests are limited to 3 scans per 24 hours. An exchange that blocks your country returns no data.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete Account. This deletes the account and the data BitLogic stores for it. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription, so cancel that in your store account first. See the data deletion page for other options.
